Common questions

How does the journey work?

Film history is split into ten chapters, one per decade from the 1930s to the 2020s. Every day the app introduces one film. The next day you answer a few questions about it, plus any older questions that are due. Reading a film's introduction to the end marks it as met.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download films the first time and to see new releases or where to watch. Films you've already downloaded, and all your questions, work offline.

How do I keep my progress when I change phones?

Everything is stored only on your iPhone, with no account. Go to Me → Settings → Backup → Export backup and save the file somewhere safe, like iCloud Drive. On the new phone, use Import backup… in the same place.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check Me → Settings → Daily reminder and make sure notifications are allowed for Mise en Scène in iOS Settings → Notifications. The reminder only arrives on days you haven't done your questions yet.

Where does the film information come from?

From The Movie Database (TMDB). Streaming availability comes from JustWatch, through TMDB. If a detail is wrong, it can be corrected on themoviedb.org.

Does the app cost anything?

No. Mise en Scène is free, with no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
