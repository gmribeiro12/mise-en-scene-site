Privacy policy

Your data stays on your iPhone

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: Mise en Scène collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you do in the app is stored only on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress through the journey, your questions and answers, the films you log (with any notes), your watchlist, the people you follow and your settings are stored in the app on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you export a backup, the app creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email…). We never see it.

What the app sends over the internet

To show films, people and posters, the app downloads public film information from The Movie Database (TMDB):

  • Film and people details are requested through a small server we run on Cloudflare. A request contains only what is needed to answer it: a film or person number, the words you type in Search, and the country used for release dates and streaming (France). The server passes the request to TMDB without your IP address, sends the answer back, and keeps no logs. Cloudflare, which hosts it, may process connection data for security as described in its privacy policy.
  • Posters and photos are downloaded directly from TMDB's image servers. Like any website, those servers receive your IP address to deliver the image. TMDB's privacy policy applies.
  • Links you open ("Where to watch", or the TMDB and JustWatch pages in About) open in an in-app browser. Those websites' own privacy policies apply.

Notifications

Reminders, the weekly digest and screening reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself. No push server is involved, and no notification data leaves your device. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

Mise en Scène is not aimed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
