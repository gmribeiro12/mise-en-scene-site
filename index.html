Mise en Scène

A daily walk through film history: one great film a day, from the 1930s to today, and the faces and names behind it.

Each day the app introduces one film from the decade you're walking through. The next day it asks you a few questions about it, together with the ones your memory schedule says are due. Little by little you get to know the directors, actors and movements of every decade.

Support · Privacy policy

Mise en Scène is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Film data comes from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming data by JustWatch.
